# Grade 3 reasoning-screen considerations: conditional design note

Status: CCAT-specific research and design only, 2026-10-05. No CCAT preparation content, book images, or test route has been imported. The school board and purchased book have not yet been identified. The user now requests a separate Vincent area with nine CCAT task types and strategy teaching; the conditional implementation specification is [U07](../resources/sol/tasks/U07-reasoning-practice.md). Original repeating and growing pattern activities remain in Vincent's Math area as general practice. Their answers are item-level observations, not an aptitude score or CCAT prediction.

## First gate: confirm the administering board's rules

The name CCAT refers here to the Grade 3 Cognitive Abilities Test/Canadian Cognitive Abilities Test, not the unrelated adult Criteria Cognitive Aptitude Test. Nelson describes CCAT 7 as measuring verbal, quantitative, and nonverbal reasoning. This is a reasoning screen, not a Grade 3 curriculum unit or a measure that LearnPilot can validly score or predict.

The Toronto District School Board's current [Universal Screening page](https://www.tdsb.on.ca/Learning-Equity-and-Well-Being/Special-Education-and-Inclusion/Universal-Screening) says no advance CCAT-7 preparation or practice is permitted and that evidence of prior exposure, preparation, or practice renders results invalid. Its [French-program guidance](https://www.tdsb.on.ca/Elementary-School/School-Choices/French-Programs/Special-Education-Inclusion-in-French-Language-Programs) says French immersion pupils take that screening in English. These are TDSB-specific facts; do not infer the learner's board from his grade and French immersion. Confirm the school board and its current instructions before creating any CCAT-labelled practice or importing the book.

If the learner is under a no-preparation rule, do not expose a CCAT practice area, timed mock tests, analogous test items, or book-derived questions before the screening. Keep ordinary Grade 3 Math, English, French, reading and low-pressure play in their existing areas, without representing them as test preparation. Ask the school or board how it interprets any already purchased or used practice material.

## Conditional path if the board permits preparation

Add an optional **Reasoning practice** area within the Grade 3 profile, separate from school Math/English/French and separate from official CCAT scoring. Use only general, original examples after review. Group them by verbal, quantitative, and nonverbal reasoning, with small sessions and explanations of *how to inspect a pattern* rather than a score prediction. Record item-level support and progress under the selected learner's ID, with labels such as “more practice” rather than ability, IQ, percentile, gifted eligibility, or likely CCAT result. [Nelson's CCAT 7 brochure](https://pages.nelson.com/assessment/downloads/CCAT-Brochure-2016.pdf) describes the three domains and sample task types; it is metadata for design, not a licence to copy official items.

Start book intake with title, publisher, edition/year, licence/usage terms if supplied, table of contents, and a small representative selection of pages only if sharing is permitted. The first review should identify topic taxonomy, question/answer structure, visuals, difficulty, and where explanations are printed. Prefer a page range or chapter reference as provenance, then author fresh original examples and manually check answers/diagrams. Avoid page-by-page transcription, bulk OCR into Git, and treating extracted answers as automatically approved. Any retained source images should be local private attachments with access control, backup/deletion and explicit rights handling; the current prototype has not implemented those controls (B16/B24–B27).

The Canadian [Copyright Act](https://laws.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-42/FullText.html) has distinct fair-dealing and private-copying provisions with conditions. Buying a book does not by itself authorize publishing its pages or questions in the public LearnPilot repository. Decide the permitted scope for any local scan or external model processing against the book's terms and applicable rules before copying or transmitting content. This note is a product boundary, not legal advice.

## Inputs needed before an implementation task

1. The administering school board, the test name/version, and any written instructions on preparation.
2. The book's exact title, publisher, edition/year, and whether it is a printed book, workbook, or licensed digital product.
3. Confirmation that a test-specific practice module is allowed. If not, keep the existing school-learning areas and do not import the book.

The conditional [U07 task card](../resources/sol/tasks/U07-reasoning-practice.md) records the proposed separate subject, teaching approach, visual-accessibility requirements, and acceptance cases. Do not expose its practice content until the board rule is resolved. Never infer an official CCAT score or approval from LearnPilot practice.
